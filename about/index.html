About

I'm Rohan Chopra. I'm from the Bay Area and I study cognitive science at UC San Diego, specializing in UX/UI design and interaction, with a minor in AI. Provost Honors, 3.92 GPA. I graduate in March 2027.

My work runs across design, film, and sound. At Mercor I evaluate AI-generated design work and write the structured critiques that teach systems what good design is. At Egnyte I ran an end-to-end research study on AI adoption that became a prioritized product roadmap. In 2022 my documentary Journey to Mars won Best STEM Film at the NASA CineSpace International Film Competition and screened on the NASA Space Station Channel. My music has passed 100,000 streams. I also design branding for the Cognitive Science Student Association at UCSD, and I built the web presence for a boutique financial advisory firm.

The through-line

People sometimes ask which one is the real job. All of them, in the sense that each taught me something the others needed. Research taught me to find out what someone actually experiences before I assume it. Film taught me sequence: what a viewer needs next, and what to cut. Music taught me hierarchy, restraint, and how to tell when something is finished. Design is where those three land on a screen. In practice I use all of it most weeks.
